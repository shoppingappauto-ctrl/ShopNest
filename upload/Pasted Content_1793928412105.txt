# 3. PRODUCT VISION

ShopNest will become a comprehensive household shopping assistant.

The primary use case:

A wife is at home while her husband shops at Walmart.

She adds milk, bread, diapers, vegetables and other items to ShopNest.

His shopping list updates instantly.

He checks items off while shopping.

She sees what has been purchased.

If he is uncertain about a product, he sends a photograph through ShopNest.

She confirms whether it is the correct product.

The app also remembers which products the family usually buys and which stores they prefer.

The same functionality must work for any household arrangement, including couples, families, roommates, relatives and caregivers.

## Long-term vision

ShopNest should eventually support:

1. Live shared shopping lists.
2. Multiple stores.
3. Product photographs and confirmations.
4. Household messaging.
5. Location-based shopping reminders.
6. Recurring household essentials.
7. Party and event planning.
8. Receipt and warranty storage.
9. Barcode scanning.
10. Shopping budgets.
11. Specialty grocery discovery.
12. Bangladeshi and halal groceries.
13. Store availability information.
14. Retailer partnerships.
15. Future scan-and-pay.
16. Household premium subscriptions.

The application must be designed for expansion without delaying the initial launch.

# 17. FUTURE SCAN-AND-PAY

This is a long-term commercial feature.

Desired workflow:

1. Shopper scans products.
2. Products are added to a digital basket.
3. Verified prices are displayed.
4. Applicable taxes and discounts are calculated.
5. Shopper confirms checkout.
6. An authorized QR or checkout token is generated.
7. A participating retailer validates the transaction.
8. Payment is completed through an approved integration.
9. A receipt is issued.

**Important:** ShopNest cannot independently introduce checkout functionality at Walmart, Costco or other retailers.

Retailer authorization, POS integrations, payment agreements and fraud controls are required.

Do not build a fake checkout system.

Keep this feature disabled until suitable partnerships exist.
